About the club

High Desert Soaring Club, Bend Oregon

High Desert Soaring Club is a volunteer-run soaring club based at Bend Municipal Airport (KBDN) in Bend, Oregon. We share a passion for safe, affordable soaring in the high-desert environment of Central Oregon.

What we do

We offer demonstration glider flights, primarily on Saturdays, and provide a pathway into glider training and ongoing club flying. Club members help with ground operations, towing, and maintenance oversight.

Operations are weather dependent and scheduled in advance, so visitors should contact us ahead of time to arrange a flight or to learn more about membership.

Our fleet

  • Schleicher ASK-21 two-seat glider (demonstration & training)
  • Grob 102 Astir CS single-seat glider
  • Pawnee tow plane

This combination allows us to introduce new people to soaring, support training, and provide solo flying opportunities for qualified members.
