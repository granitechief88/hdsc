Our fleet

Gliders and tow plane at High Desert Soaring Club

Our fleet is configured to support demonstration flights, training, and solo soaring in the high-desert environment around Bend, Oregon.

Schleicher ASK-21 (two-seat)

The ASK-21 is our primary two-seat training and demonstration glider. With a comfortable cockpit, excellent visibility, and forgiving handling, it is ideal for introductory flights and instruction.

Role: Demonstration flights, dual instruction, and currency checks.

Grob 102 Astir CS (single-seat)

The Grob 102 Astir CS is a single-seat fiberglass glider used by qualified members for solo soaring. It offers good performance for local soaring and exploring thermals around Central Oregon.

Role: Solo soaring for current and appropriately endorsed pilots.

Pawnee tow plane

Our Pawnee tow plane provides reliable aerotows from Bend Municipal Airport (KBDN) to soaring altitude. Tow pilots and ground crews work together to ensure safe, efficient launch operations.

Role: Aerotow launches for all club flights.

Additional aircraft may occasionally be present for visiting pilots or special events.

For questions about aircraft seating, weight and balance, or medical considerations, please contact the club before scheduling a flight.
