Fly with High Desert Soaring Club

We operate primarily on Saturdays (weather permitting) out of Bend Municipal Airport (KBDN) in Bend, Oregon. Demonstration flights are offered by prior arrangement.

Flying days

Demonstration flights are typically scheduled on Saturdays, weather permitting. Please contact us in advance to discuss availability and schedule a flight time.

Scheduling a demonstration flight

To request a demonstration flight, send us an email with your preferred Saturday, the number of people in your group, and any previous flying experience. A club volunteer will reply to confirm details and answer questions.

  • Flights are weather and volunteer availability dependent.
  • Operations take place at Bend Municipal Airport (KBDN).
  • Please arrive early to allow time for a safety briefing.
