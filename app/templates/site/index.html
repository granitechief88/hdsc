Ways to get involved at High Desert Soaring Club

Whether you're curious about gliding, working toward a pilot certificate, or already an experienced soaring pilot, there's a place for you in our club.

Demonstration flights

Take a demonstration ride in our ASK-21 two-seat glider with one of our experienced pilots. Flights typically operate on Saturdays, weather permitting.

Learn to soar

Continue from demonstration flights into structured training toward your glider rating. Benefit from instruction, ground briefings, and guidance from club members.

Join the club

Become part of a friendly soaring community based at Bend Municipal Airport. Help with operations, share the tow plane, and enjoy the high-desert soaring environment.

Our fleet

Gliders and tow plane

High Desert Soaring Club currently operates a two-seat ASK-21 for demonstration and training flights, a single-seat Grob 102 Astir CS, and a Pawnee tow plane.

Schleicher ASK-21 (two-seat)

Our primary two-seat glider for demonstration flights and training. Offers excellent visibility and predictable handling for new pilots.

Grob 102 Astir CS (single-seat)

A single-seat performance glider for solo soaring once members are qualified. Ideal for local soaring and exploring Central Oregon lift.

Pawnee tow plane

Our Pawnee tow plane provides reliable aerotows to soaring altitude, launching club flights from Bend Municipal Airport.
